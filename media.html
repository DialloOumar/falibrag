Groundbreaking: Extension & Modernization of Université de Kindia

On March 25, 2026, Dr. Diaka Sidibé, Minister of Higher Education and Scientific Research, laid the foundation stone for the extension and modernization of the Université de Kindia. Falibra Group SARL is the main contractor for Lot 1, which includes an administrative building (R+4), a cafeteria for 1,000 persons, a modern infirmary, sports facilities, and student housing for 2,700+ students. This project is part of Guinea's Simandou 2040 program under President Mamadi Doumbouya.

Falibra Group: Main Contractor for Université de Kindia Extension

Dr. Diaka Sidibé, Minister of Higher Education and Scientific Research, laid the foundation stone for the extension and modernization of the Université de Kindia on March 25, 2026. Falibra Group SARL leads Lot 1, covering an R+4 administrative building, a 1,000-seat cafeteria, a modern infirmary, sports facilities, and student housing for over 2,700 students — with 40% of spaces reserved for women. The project is part of Guinea's Simandou 2040 program.
